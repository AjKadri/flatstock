Visibility: once a project is approved, its name, one-liner, categories, team and updates are public. Description, demo and live link go public at the lock, and the rest stays with the judges.

## 6. Judging mechanics

1. The organisers screen every submission; approved ones go to the vote. They can disqualify at any stage for cheating, plagiarism, or faked transactions or proof.
2. Round one: judges vote for projects they like. The community votes with $WIF (1 WIF = 1 vote, burned) and counts as **one judge**. The top 10 by votes are finalists; if 10 or fewer are approved, all of them go to the final.
3. Final: judges and community vote again, and the most votes wins. Judges will try the live app.
4. Judges: ogle (@cryptogle), niro (@cryptoape), daniel zou (@blueshirt666), glabuz (@glabuz, Ava Labs gaming/retail BD), sahil sen (@sensahil, QuickNode devrel), plus the World is Flat team (@wifonrh). "More judges soon."
5. Decisions are final.
