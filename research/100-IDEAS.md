# 100 ideas from 5 points of view: World is Flat hackathon
29 Oct 2026. Every idea is one plain sentence, with how $WIF is used in brackets. All are unchecked: none has been tested against the rules or feasibility yet.
"A blockchain" is read as **the chain team** (how Robinhood Chain itself would approach it).

---

## 1. The senior developer
**Approach:** "What can I ship solidly in 4 days that judges can click and that won't break?"
- Starts from the riskiest integration and cuts scope hard.
- Picks things with clear on-chain proof.
- Prefers small and finished over big and half-done.

1. Pay gas with $WIF on Robinhood Chain, so you never need ETH (a paymaster; $WIF is the gas).
2. Swap any Stock Token in one tap, gas paid in $WIF (fee paid in $WIF, part burned).
3. Send stocks to anyone by phone number or X handle (sender pays in $WIF).
4. Schedule a stock buy every week automatically (each run paid in $WIF).
5. Split one payment into five stocks at once (a basket router; fee in $WIF).
6. A stop-loss that fires even when you're offline (keepers stake $WIF to run it).
7. A Telegram bot that buys stocks for you by chat (commands paid in $WIF).
8. A wallet that shows your stocks with real dividends and splits explained ($WIF unlocks pro view).
9. Batch-send $WIF or stocks to 100 people in one transaction (fee burned).
10. A widget any site can embed to sell a Stock Token (sites stake $WIF to be listed).
11. A "proof of holding" badge: show you own NVDA without revealing your wallet (mint costs $WIF).
12. One-click "zap" from $WIF straight into any stock (route through $WIF, part burned).
13. Recurring payroll that pays staff in stocks (employer locks $WIF as a bond).
14. A price-alert service for Stock Tokens (lock $WIF to keep alerts on).
15. An on-chain limit order for stocks (fill bounty paid in $WIF).
16. Stock-for-stock swaps with no middle step, e.g. AAPL→NVDA (fee in $WIF).
17. Gift cards that turn into real stocks when redeemed (gift fee burned).
18. A safe "allowance" wallet: a parent funds, the teen can only buy stocks (parent locks $WIF).
19. An SDK so any app adds "buy stocks" in five lines (apps stake $WIF for API keys).
20. A public explorer page for every Stock Token (ad slots bought with $WIF).

## What each would probably pick
| Persona | Likely pick | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Senior dev | **Pay gas with $WIF** or **Telegram stock bot** | Small, finished, judges can click it |
| Researcher | **Track-record fund** or **weekend price oracle** | Evidenced gaps, honest proof |
| KOL | **Best stock picker on CT, proven on-chain** or **community ETF** | Viral, fits the organiser's "GOAT of CT" history |
| Founder | **Savings circle in US stocks** or **US stocks for Africans** | A real customer who comes back |
| Chain team | **60-second onboarding** or **keeper network** | Grows the chain, uses its newest pieces |

Repeat appearances across personas usually mean a strong idea:
- **Proven stock pickers** (researcher, KOL, founder): that's the tipster or fund family.
- **Community-chosen basket** (researcher, KOL, founder): the basket family.
- **Savings in stocks with friends** (founder, KOL streaks).
