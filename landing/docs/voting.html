Voting

Rounds

A round has a list of stocks (three today: AAPL, NVDA, MSFT), an opening time and a closing time. The app always votes on the open round. After a round closes, its mix is frozen and used for buying.

Casting a vote

  • One vote per wallet per round. A second attempt is rejected, and the app tells you so before spending anything.
  • Each vote burns exactly 1 $WIF, permanently.
  • The first vote on a contract may ask for a one-time $WIF approval.
  • With the 10 $WIF lock, you sign the vote and the relayer pays the gas. Without it, your wallet sends the transaction and you pay the ETH gas.
  • Signed votes expire after 15 minutes.

How the mix is computed

The mix is a blend: 50% equal weight + 50% vote share, then no stock may exceed 50% (any excess is redistributed to the others). With zero votes, every stock gets an equal share.

Example (3 stocks, 1 vote for AAPL)Equal part (50%)Vote part (50%)Result
AAPL16.67%50.00%66.67% → capped at 50%
NVDA16.67%0%25%
MSFT16.67%0%25%

The cap-excess goes to the other stocks equally, which is why round 4 shows 50 / 25 / 25.

Closing

After a round's closing time, anyone can call closeRound on the contract. That stores the final weights. A closed round cannot be voted on again. Rounds on the current contract start at Round 4; earlier rounds ran on retired test contracts and are listed separately under "Test rounds".

Past rounds

The app (More tab) and the landing page show each round's final mix, votes and $WIF burned, read straight from the chain, with Blockscout links.
