Privacy

Draft for the project team to review before submission.

What we ask for

No name, email or account. You connect a wallet and sign.

What our servers see

  • When you use the app, requests go to our RPC proxy and relayer API. They see your IP address, your wallet address (for the daily cap) and the requests you make. Daily usage counters are kept in memory. Standard web server logs may keep IP addresses for a limited time.
  • On-chain data (votes, buys, sells, locks) is public by nature.

Third parties

  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
  • The landing page reads public data directly from the Robinhood Chain RPC.
  • Links to Blockscout and other sites are governed by their own policies.

No tracking

We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on the app or the landing page.
