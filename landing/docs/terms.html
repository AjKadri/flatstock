1. What this is

Flatstock is a hackathon project that helps you buy a community-voted mix of Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain. It is provided as is, without warranties.

2. Your responsibility

You are responsible for your wallet, your keys and your signatures, and for following the laws where you live. You must not use Flatstock if you are in, or are, a person from a restricted or prohibited jurisdiction listed in Eligibility and risks.

3. No advice

Nothing here is investment, legal or tax advice.

4. Risk

Smart contracts and markets carry risk, including total loss. The software is unaudited. Votes burn $WIF permanently and cannot be reversed.

5. Availability

The app, the relayer and gas sponsorship may be paused, limited or stopped at any time. You can always use ordinary wallet transactions with the public contracts.

6. Independence

Flatstock is independent and not affiliated with Robinhood.

7. Open source

The code is Apache-2.0 licensed in the repository.
