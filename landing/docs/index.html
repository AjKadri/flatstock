Overview

Flatstock lets you buy the US stocks the community picked, in one tap, with no gas. $WIF holders vote each round on a three-stock mix; you buy that mix into your own wallet on Robinhood Chain.

The idea in four lines

  1. Vote. Each vote burns 1 $WIF and nudges next week's mix toward your pick.
  2. Close. When a round closes, its mix is final.
  3. Buy. One signature buys the final mix of the most recent closed round. Stock Tokens go straight to your wallet.
  4. Hold or sell. They are yours. Sell back to USDG any time.

This week and next week

Buying uses the final mix of the most recent closed round ("This week's mix, Round N, final"). Voting always happens on the open round ("Vote for next week, Round N+1"). Rounds are closed and opened back to back, so buying and voting do not stop. Before the very first round has closed, the app shows a clearly labelled live preview instead.

Weekly schedule

Rounds close every Monday at 15:00 WAT (Lagos time). At that moment the round's mix becomes final, buying switches to it for the week, and the next round opens for voting. Round 4 closes Mon 9 Nov 15:00 WAT (3 stocks). Round 5 has 8 stocks and closes Mon 16 Nov 15:00 WAT.

Example: "This week: Round 4 final mix (3 stocks). Voting now: Round 5, 8 stocks, for next week."

What it is not

Flatstock is a hackathon project: it has not been audited, it is independent and not affiliated with Robinhood, and it does not give investment advice. See Eligibility and risks.
