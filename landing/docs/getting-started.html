Getting started

1. A wallet

Use any browser wallet that supports custom networks (the app also works inside wallet in-app browsers). Self-custody only: Flatstock never holds your funds.

2. Add Robinhood Chain

The app has an "Add Robinhood Chain" button (More tab, or the wrong-network banner). To add it by hand:

FieldValue
Network nameRobinhood Chain
Chain ID4663
RPC URLhttps://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com
CurrencyETH
Explorerhttps://robinhoodchain.blockscout.com

3. What you need to hold

  • USDG to buy with (0x5fc5360D0400a0Fd4f2af552ADD042D716F1d168).
  • $WIF to vote (0x5173b8eA6923c2d40267890D45C46a3480954773). Each vote burns 1.
  • ETH for gas, unless your wallet has the 10 $WIF lock.

Flatstock does not sell these. Get them on Robinhood Chain through the venue you prefer.

4. First steps

  1. Open the app and connect.
  2. Check Home for this week's mix and the round countdown.
  3. Optional: Vote for next week, and lock 10 $WIF for free gas.
  4. Buy: enter an amount, check prices, review, sign.

Stock Tokens are not available to everyone. Read Eligibility and risks first.
