Fees

Flatstock fee: 0%

Flatstock takes no trading fee today. The fee switch is off.

What you do pay

  • The swap price, including the Uniswap pool fee of each stock's pool (0.05% for AAPL, NVDA, GOOGL and SPY; 0.3% for MSFT, AMZN, META and TSLA) and market spread, which the price guard keeps within 2% of Chainlink.
  • ETH gas for your own transactions: the one-time approvals, the lock transaction, and any action when you have no lock. With the lock, votes and buys are sponsored (up to 3 per day).
  • 1 $WIF burned per vote.

Relayer costs measured on a fork of the real pools at the live gas price: about 0.0000084 ETH for a 2-stock gasless buy, 0.0000123 for 4 stocks and 0.0000223 for 8 stocks, and about 0.0000067 for a gasless sell. A vote costs about 0.000003 ETH. Minimum buy: 5 USDG.
