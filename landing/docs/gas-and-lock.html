Gas and the 10 $WIF lock

The idea

Lock 10 $WIF and the relayer pays the gas for your votes and buys. The lock is your own $WIF, held in the lock contract (0x2421E6F3fbaAFB96e0987208a1f3AC51F46EE121); it has no owner and cannot move one user's balance to another account.

Limits

  • Up to 3 sponsored transactions per wallet per day (the day follows Lagos time). A vote or buy uses 1, a sell uses 2 (permit plus sell).
  • Sponsorship pauses automatically if the relayer's ETH balance falls below 0.0003 ETH, and there is a kill switch. When paused, the app says so and you can still buy and pay your own gas.
  • The relayer can only submit exactly what you signed.
  • The smallest buy the relayer will sponsor is 5 USDG.

Locking

In the app: Portfolio → Free gas → "Lock 10 $WIF for free gas". Your wallet asks for a $WIF approval of exactly 10 $WIF and then the lock transaction (you pay that gas).

Unlocking

The lock contract has an unlock(amount) function: you can take back all of your locked $WIF, or part of it as long as what remains is still at least 10 $WIF. In the app, Portfolio → Free gas → Unlock returns all of your locked $WIF after a confirmation, and free gas turns off until you lock 10 $WIF again. You can unlock any time: there is no cooldown and no fee. Misuse is bounded by the 3-a-day cap per wallet, the cost of every sponsored action, and the automatic pause when the relayer's ETH is low. A minimum lock age could be added in the relayer later without changing any contract.

Without a lock

Nothing breaks. Every action is a normal wallet transaction and you pay the small ETH gas.
