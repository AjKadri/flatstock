Eligibility and risks

Availability

Not available to US persons, or in Canada, the UK, Switzerland or other restricted jurisdictions. Not investment advice. Restricted jurisdictions

Stock Tokens are restricted for the United States and U.S. persons, Canada, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, and prohibited for Cuba, Belarus, Iran, North Korea, Russia, Syria, Ukraine, South Sudan, Sudan, Myanmar and Venezuela. Source: docs.robinhood.com/rhj/restricted-jurisdictions, checked 5 Nov 2026. The list may change. You are responsible for following the rules where you live.

Not investment advice

Flatstock shows a community-voted mix. It is not a recommendation, and stock prices can fall. You can lose money.

Technical risks

  • Hackathon software, not audited. Smart contracts can have bugs.
  • Prices come from onchain pools; the guard blocks trades more than 2% from Chainlink but cannot remove market risk.
  • Stock Tokens trade against USDG on-chain, and liquidity can be thin, especially outside US market hours.
  • The relayer is run by one operator and can be paused or run out of gas funds. You can always use normal wallet transactions instead.

Independence

Flatstock is independent and not affiliated with Robinhood. "Robinhood Chain" is used only to name the network.
