How it works

Three steps on one line.

  1. 1Vote

    Pick the stock you want more of in next week's mix. One vote per wallet per round, and each vote burns 1 $WIF.

  2. 2Buy the mix

    One signature buys the final mix of the last closed round. Or untick stocks and buy a smaller custom set (at least 2, $5 minimum). Stock Tokens go straight to your wallet.

  3. 3Hold

    Your shares, your keys. Sell back to USDG any time. Dividends and splits update your share count by themselves.

Why $WIF

$WIF picks the basket and pays the gas.

World is Fair-style voting: one wallet, one vote, 1 $WIF burned — counted and burned on-chain by our contract, in the same transaction as the vote. How voting works

  • Every vote burns 1 $WIF.Voting costs a real $WIF, permanently removed from supply.
  • Lock 10 $WIF, skip the gas.Locked wallets get sponsored votes and buys, up to 3 a day. No lock? You pay the small ETH gas yourself.
  • Holders choose, not a desk.Half the mix follows the vote, half stays equal-weight, and no stock can exceed 50%.

Safety

Built so it can only do what you signed.

  • Self-custodyStock Tokens are sent to your own wallet. The app never holds your funds.
  • A signature for every actionVotes, buys and sells each need your own signature for that exact action. The relayer can only submit what you signed.
  • Price guardEvery swap must be within 2% of the Chainlink price. If any leg fails, the whole buy is blocked.
  • Fresh oracles onlyStale or missing prices stop the buy. The system fails closed.
  • Caps and a kill switchSponsored gas is capped at 3 actions per wallet per day, pauses when the relayer is low on ETH, and has a kill switch.
  • Open contractsThe votes and router contracts are verified on Blockscout and the source is public. Not audited: see the FAQ.

FAQ

Quick answers.

What do I need to start?

A wallet with Robinhood Chain added, some USDG to buy with, and $WIF if you want to vote. Without a 10 $WIF lock you also need a little ETH for gas.

Can I vote more than once?

One vote per wallet per round, and each vote burns 1 $WIF.

Which round does my buy use?

The final mix of the most recent closed round. Voting runs on the open round, which sets next week's mix.

What does it cost?

0% Flatstock fee today. You pay the swap price, plus gas unless your wallet has a 10 $WIF lock. The minimum buy is $5.

Is it available everywhere?

No. Stock Tokens are restricted for the US and US persons, Canada, the UK and Switzerland, and prohibited in several other countries. See the notice in the footer. This is not investment advice.

Is it audited?

No. Flatstock has not had an independent security audit, and a hackathon build can still have bugs, so use small amounts. What has been done: the contracts have automated tests (Foundry), the relayer has unit tests, and trades were tested on a fork of the real chain with real pools (2, 4 and 8-stock gasless buys, a gasless sell, and the vote-weighting formula against the real contract). Each mainnet test is recorded with its transaction in the repository. The votes and router contracts are verified on Blockscout and the source is public. Security model · Test log
