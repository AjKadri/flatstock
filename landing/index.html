Why $WIF

$WIF decides the basket and pays for the gas.

  • Every vote burns 1 $WIF.Voting costs a real $WIF, permanently removed from supply.
  • Lock 10 $WIF, skip the gas.Locked wallets get sponsored votes and buys, up to 3 per day. No lock? You just pay the small ETH gas yourself.
  • Holders choose, not a desk.Half the mix follows the vote, half stays equal-weight, and no stock can exceed 50%.

Safety

Built so it can only do what you signed.

Self-custody

Stock Tokens are sent to your own wallet. The app never holds your funds.

A signature for every action

Votes, buys and sells each need your own signature for that exact action. The relayer can only submit what you signed.

Price guard

Every swap must be within 2% of the Chainlink price. If any leg fails, the whole buy is blocked.

Fresh oracles only

Stale or missing prices stop the buy. The system fails closed.

Caps and a kill switch

Sponsored gas is capped at 3 actions per wallet per day, pauses when the relayer is low on ETH, and has a kill switch.

Open contracts

The votes and router contracts are verified on Blockscout and the source is public. This is a hackathon build and has not been audited.

FAQ

Quick answers

What do I need to start?

A wallet with Robinhood Chain added, some USDG to buy with, and $WIF if you want to vote. Without a 10 $WIF lock you also need a little ETH for gas.

Can I vote more than once?

No. Each wallet gets one vote per round, and each vote burns 1 $WIF.

Which round does my buy use?

The final mix of the most recent closed round. Voting runs on the open round, which sets next week's mix.

What does it cost?

0% Flatstock fee today. You pay the swap price, plus gas unless your wallet has a 10 $WIF lock.

Is it available everywhere?

No. Stock Tokens are restricted for the US and US persons, Canada, the UK and Switzerland, and prohibited in several other countries. See the notice below. This is not investment advice.

Is it audited?

No. It is a hackathon project. The source is public and the main contracts are verified, but use small amounts.
